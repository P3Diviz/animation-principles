The principle

Timing is how many frames an action takes: more feels slower and heavier, fewer feels lighter. On twos, each drawing is held for two frames: 12 drawings a second instead of 25.

What to notice

Fewer frames feels light and fast; more frames feels heavy or slow.

In After Effects

Move keyframes closer or further apart. Alt+drag scales a group of keys.
