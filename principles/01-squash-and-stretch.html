Squash and stretch

Material

Rubber ball: 100% squash, 25 frames up and down.

The dashed circle is the ball at rest. Squashed, it covers about the same area.

The principle

Squash and stretch gives a drawing weight and flexibility. A soft object flattens when it hits something and stretches when it moves fast, but its volume stays the same.

What to notice

Squash on impact, stretch at speed, same volume throughout.

In After Effects

Scale with the link off, keyed at contact and just after.
