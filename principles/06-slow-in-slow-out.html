Presets

The principle

Things speed up gradually as they start and slow down as they stop. That means more frames near the keys and fewer in the middle. In After Effects this is easing, shaped in the Graph Editor.

What to notice

Marks bunch up where it's slow and spread out where it's fast.

In After Effects

Select keys, F9 for Easy Ease. Shift+F3 opens the Graph Editor.

Try this

Drag a handle above the box to make it overshoot and settle back.
