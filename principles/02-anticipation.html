Anticipation

Action

Wind-up

The highlighted part of the timeline is the wind-up.

The principle

Anticipation is the small move that prepares the audience for the main one: a crouch before a jump, a wind-up before a throw. It goes the opposite way to the action, so the action reads clearly and feels powerful. Without it, moves look sudden and weightless.

What to notice

The small move the opposite way first.

In After Effects

An extra keyframe just before the main move, pulling back.

Try this

Make the wind-up so big it becomes funny.
