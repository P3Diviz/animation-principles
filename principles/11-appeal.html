11Appeal

Shape language

Round reads friendly, square sturdy, pointed sharp or less trustworthy.

Proportions

Pose

Same dragon, two ways. Which is more appealing?

The principle

Appeal is what makes a character pleasing to watch, hero or villain: clear shape language, readable proportions and a strong pose. A bigger face and eyes read younger and cuter.

What to notice

Clear shapes, readable silhouettes and personality.

In After Effects

Shape layers: rounded corners, consistent stroke widths.
