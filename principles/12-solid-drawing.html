12Solid drawing

Drawing

Stick figure, then simple solids, then flat colour, then light, shade and texture.

Turn

Pose

The principle

Solid drawing means treating a character as a 3D form with weight and volume, not a flat outline. Build it from simple solids, wrap centre lines round them, and avoid twinning, where both sides mirror each other exactly.

What to notice

Things have weight, volume and a back as well as a front.

In After Effects

3D layers and the camera show form from new angles.
